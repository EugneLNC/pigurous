# Provenance and third-party material

The repository contains project source, tests and hand-authored deterministic renderer fixtures. The README gallery and GIF are generated by `scripts/make_public_demo.py`; they contain no downloaded music, charts, textures or pretrained weights and are explicitly not trained-model demonstrations.

NumPy, SciPy, Pillow, PyTorch, Three.js, Vite and optional audio/model libraries retain their respective upstream licenses. Installing a dependency does not relicense it under this project's code license.

The original research used separately downloaded Phigros/Phira chart and audio material and a frozen MERT encoder. These datasets and pretrained weights are not redistributed here. Public availability does not grant redistribution or commercial-training rights. Obtain material independently under applicable permissions; follow the provenance manifests generated by the download/preparation scripts.

Selected research checkpoints and human preference records are retained privately outside this public source release. Historical experiment documents describe completed runs and failed/pending checks; their datasets, artifacts and hardware-specific paths are not bundled.
